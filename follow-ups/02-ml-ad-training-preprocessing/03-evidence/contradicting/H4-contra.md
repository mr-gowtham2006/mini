## Contra evidence (fires at H4)
- **C1 — TranAD §3.2 global min-max, VERIFIED (`https://www.vldb.org/pvldb/vol15/p1201-tuli.pdf`, Eq.1):** *"min(T) and max(T) are the mode wise minimum and maximum vectors in the training time-series"* — one GLOBAL norm over the full training trace, feeding the SOTA line (avg F1 0.8802, +17.06%). Every deep baseline TranAD beats (GDN, MTAD-GAT, USAD, OmniAnomaly) trains under the same shared global-norm regime. If per-machine scope were decisive, the entire SOTA leaderboard would not sit on global min-max. Weight: HIGH (direct precedent; PA discount does not touch preprocessing scope).
- **C2 — Scale carries the anomaly signal; per-unit norms erase it:** "On the Role of RevIN" (`https://arxiv.org/html/2603.11869v1`): *"by removing scale and offset from the input, RevIN discards potentially predictive context"*; "Be Wary of Your Time Series Preprocessing" (`https://arxiv.org/html/2602.17568`): *"if the downstream task depends heavily on identifying large variations in a specific channel, such as in anomaly detection, where a spike in one sensor may signal [an anomaly]"* global scope preserves it. Twin faults ARE magnitude events (4–7σ); per-machine robust norms re-center each machine's spikes toward its own IQR, compressing exactly the cross-machine magnitude contrast the detector should score. The RevIN-Anomaly-Transformer paper (`https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10675229/`) concedes the point structurally: associations on normalized data but PRIORS on original non-normalized data. Weight: MEDIUM-HIGH.
- **C3 — Thin-pool estimation noise (twin-specific arithmetic, literature-backed):** per-machine median/IQR is fit on RUN-normal-only inside folds ≈ ~200 machine-steps/machine/episode; small-sample scale estimates are high-variance (isixsigma IQR/1.55 study; RevIN's mean/std *"known to be"* fragile per 2510.04667; per-unit unreliability analog in RNA-seq dispersion, PMC3654711). Global-per-channel pools are 32× larger. Noise in M's denominators becomes F1 variance concentrated on minority classes — the opposite of Prediction 2. Weight: MEDIUM (arithmetic + analogs, no direct TSAD ablation).
- **C4 — Transferability of shared/global statistics:** GDformer (`https://arxiv.org/html/2501.18196v2`): frozen GLOBAL dictionary/prototypes transfer across datasets with *"little F1-score reduction,"* *"normal points may have shared [structure]"*; MADDoC transfer framework (`https://doi.org/10.1145/3620678.3624655`) superior Precision/F1 via shared modeling. Shared operating points across twin machines (same line physics, class-scaled) predict G-side strength. Weight: MEDIUM-LOW (representation transfer, not norm-scope ablation).
- **C5 — No universal scope winner in ablations:** MTSC input-transformation study (`https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2210.07713`): MinMax/Quantile lead for deep models, Robust/Standard deviate by classifier — scope×backbone interaction is the empirical norm, matching H4-A2 more than H4-main. "Large Comparison" (BDR 2023, `https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bdr.2023.100407`): max-absolute-scale ≈ z-norm rival. Nobody in the retrieved literature runs per-machine-robust-vs-global-min-max head-to-head — consistent with H4's "open gap" framing, and it means H4-main has zero direct precedent. Weight: MEDIUM (for the wash branch).

## Supporting-side hits found (disclosed, not suppressed)
