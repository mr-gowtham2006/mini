## Contra evidence (fires at H5)
- **C1 — POT's calibration hunger vs twin pools:** SPOT initialization fits GPD on the excess pile of an **n ∼ 1000** calibration batch (Siffer et al., KDD 2017, `https://www.eecs.yorku.ca/course_archive/2018-19/F/6412/reading/kdd17p1067.pdf`); twin calibration-normals are **~300/episode** — sub-SPOT-scale by ~3×. GPD tail fits on dozens of peaks have high estimator variance (threshold-selection literature, Q5: consistency needs intermediate sequences `nu/n → 0`; bootstrap-uncertainty machinery in 2310.17999 exists because the point estimate is unstable). Predicted M0b behavior: POT arm degrades toward Pct-level F1 with the highest cross-budget variance (H5 Prediction 1 concedes the variance; contra says the variance eats the F1 edge too). Weight: MEDIUM-HIGH (design-spec numbers vs twin arithmetic).
- **C2 — Stationarity wall:** SPOT *"works only on stationary data"* (libspot guide, `https://asiffer.github.io/libspot/guides/drifting/`); DSPOT handles only linear-drift-relative gaps, not knee-drift; SCS/MACS (2025, `https://arxiv.org/html/2508.06638v1`): *"Percentile-based approaches… falter under persistent distributional drift… EVT-POT… assume the threshold regime is quasi-stationary."* Twin episodes contain wear-drift + STARVED regimes → POT's core assumption fails where H5 most needs separation (Prediction 3's wear-drift subset). Weight: MEDIUM.
- **C3 — Calibration-data-dominance (the H0 mechanism):** "Threshold Paradox" (2026, `https://www.scitepress.org/Papers/2026/143926/143926.pdf`): test-calibrated thresholding injects supervision into unsupervised eval — i.e., *which data* the threshold touches dominates *which method* computes it. SCAR study (`https://arxiv.org/html/2405.00704`): global-vs-segment threshold choice moves AUC substantially under drift; score ranges shift across periods so *"a global threshold may lead to many false positives"* — method-level differences drown in calibration-regime differences. All three H5 arms fit on the SAME clean-episode validation → shared calibration may compress arm spreads inside the wire. Weight: MEDIUM.
- **C4 — GG-payoff doubt on twin-shaped data:** "Anomalies in MTS benchmarks are mostly univariate" (2026, `https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2606.02670`): strictly cross-channel anomalies *"essentially non-existent"* across 8 benchmarks — dependence-correction machinery (GG's χ² leg) has little to correct where univariate signals dominate. Twin faults are per-machine rectangular offsets: GG ≈ Pct + 10× compute → H5-A3 (tax without gain) is the live branch. Weight: MEDIUM-LOW (benchmark-scope, twin-analog).
- **C5 — Protocol-dominance precedent:** AAAI'22 rigorous-eval (`https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/download/20680/20439`) + balanced-F1 line (`https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2409.13053`): reported F1 differences across papers are dominated by PA/test-threshold protocol choices, not detector/threshold-method substance. Under H5's locked protocol (PA-off, validation-only fits), published arm gaps are expected to shrink — directionally toward the collapse wire. Weight: MEDIUM-LOW (meta-argument, not an arm comparison).
