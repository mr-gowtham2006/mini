## Ranked gaps (novelty × feasibility, high → low)

### G1 — Latency-SLO streaming RCA + explanation for flow lines (RANK 1)
- **Mechanism (3 lines):** Replace batch PCMCI+PyRCA with an incremental causal layer (sliding-window conditional-independence updates + cached graph skeleton) feeding an anytime ranked walk that returns a top-k trace within a hard deadline (e.g., 100 ms–1 s SimPy-time). Explanation degrades gracefully: deadline hit → return current ranking + coverage flag instead of blocking actuation. Latency, recall@k, and explanation-completeness are co-reported per incident, so speed/quality trade-offs are measured, not asserted.
- **Closest art + why it doesn't fill it:** ST-GraphRCA-class work shows the split explicitly — offline models (Mulan) exceed 1500 ms by architecture and even online OCEAN spikes to ~1850 ms under CPU overload, i.e. neither meets ms-scale actuation budgets; the 6G twin result (97.7% bearing-fault classification at 0.8 ms end-to-end, 15.6× over WiFi-6/5.25× over 5G) is classification-only, with no causal trace or explanation attached; PCMCI itself is batch and minute-scale in practice (F-PCMCI reports ~2–9 min runs). Nobody publishes faster-than-real-time *RCA + ranked explanation* with a latency SLO on a flow line.
- **Semester build:** Add `streaming_rca.py` beside the batch path: tumbling/sliding windows over the 22-machine event stream, incremental edge re-test only on drift-flagged node pairs, anytime PyRCA walk with deadline parameter; benchmark harness replays seeded multi-fault scenarios and plots recall@1/3 vs deadline (10 ms–60 s) plus fallback rate; A1–A7 gate consumes the partial trace with the coverage flag. Success = recall@3 ≥ batch baseline at 1 s deadline with p99 < deadline.
- **Falsification tripwire:** This gap closes if a 2026+ paper or artifact publishes incrementally-updated causal RCA on a discrete manufacturing line with p99 end-to-end (detect→ranked explanation) under 100 ms *and* recall within 5 pts of its batch equivalent.

### G2 — Energy/cost-aware recovery actions, kWh-per-reroute (RANK 2)
- **Mechanism (3 lines):** Score every candidate corrective action (reroute via redundant station, rework loop, scrap-and-replenish, AGV re-dispatch, idle-and-wait) with a kWh + tariff cost model grounded in Verdandi CH9 energy-per-unit metering, alongside success probability. The A1–A7 gate / masked-PPO reward becomes bi-objective: expected recovery value minus expected energy cost under ISO 50001 EnPI accounting. Decisions log kWh-per-recovery so cost, not just success, is auditable per incident.
- **Closest art + why it doesn't fill it:** Energy-aware scheduling is mature (EAFJSP MILP under TOU pricing + PV/battery; PPO-based DRL multi-energy schedulers cutting annual energy cost ~16%; tariff-aware RL load-shifting ~10% savings; steel-plant 32% energy-cost case studies) and ISO 50001 corrective-action procedure exists — but all of it optimizes *production schedules / steady-state consumption*, never the *post-fault recovery choice* conditioned on a diagnosis (which reroute costs 2× kWh for 5% more success?). No agent trades recovery success against recovery cost.
- **Semester build:** Energy ledger module (per-station kW states: run/idle/heat-up + AGV Wh/m) calibrated to CH9 spec; extend seeded replay so each corrective action emits kWh and tariff cost; A/B the gate: success-only vs success-minus-cost reward on a scenario suite with peak/off-peak variants; report Pareto frontier (recovery rate vs kWh/recovery) + ISO 50001-style EnPI delta. Success = cost-aware policy saves ≥15% kWh/recovery at ≤2 pts recovery-rate loss.
- **Falsification tripwire:** This gap closes if any published agent or vendor artifact demonstrates diagnosis-conditioned recovery selection minimizing kWh or tariff cost (not schedule cost) with measured kWh-per-recovery on a discrete line.

### G3 — Cross-domain transfer lathe→flow with measured degradation curves (RANK 3)
- **Mechanism (3 lines):** Train detectors/RCA components on causRCA lathe data (170 normal + 100 hardware-in-the-loop fault recordings, 92-node expert graph) and evaluate zero-/few-shot on Verdandi's flow-line SimPy twin, sweeping adaptation budget (0, 5, 20, 50 target-domain samples). Report degradation curves: AUROC / causal-edge F1 / recall@k vs samples, plus a domain-gap predictor (which fault classes transfer, which collapse). Negative transfer is a first-class result, not a failure to hide.
- **Closest art + why it doesn't fill it:** causRCA (CIRPe 2025, Zenodo, 92 nodes/104 edges, probe/hydraulics/coolant subgraphs) enables joint CD+RCA benchmarking but only *within* the lathe domain; the only demonstrated transfer is same-model face-turning OK-reference transfer between near-identical machines (direction- and pair-dependent, false-alarm-sensitive) — i.e. same-physics, same-topology. No work transfers detectors or RCA across physical domains (rotating-machine lathe → discrete flow line) with quantified degradation.
- **Semester build:** Feature-alignment shim (resample lathe channels ↔ flow-line sensor/process variables onto a shared event vocabulary); run transfer matrix (lathe→flow, flow→lathe sanity, same-domain controls); plot degradation curves + per-fault-class transfer table; ship the shim + curves as a FactoryRCA-Bench extension task. Success = monotonic sample-efficiency curves + identification of ≥1 transferable and ≥1 non-transferable fault class with mechanism notes.
- **Falsification tripwire:** This gap closes if a 2026+ publication or benchmark reports cross-physical-domain detector/RCA transfer (machining ↔ flow/discrete line) with sample-efficiency or degradation curves.

### G4 — Formal safety shield on flow-line corrective actions (RANK 4)
- **Mechanism (3 lines):** Interpose a verified shield between masked-PPO/A1–A7 outputs and SimPy actuation: each proposed action (reroute, rework inject, AGV dispatch, release) is projected through hard constraints (station capacity, thermal/safety interlocks, AGV collision envelopes, no-release-on-uninspected) encoded as MILP/zonotope reachability checks. Unsafe actions are minimally corrected (nearest feasible action) and every override is logged with the violated invariant. Shield latency is budgeted inside the control loop.
- **Closest art + why it doesn't fill it:** Shield theory is solid (Alshiekh-style runtime shields, CACM shield synthesis, ProSh probabilistic risk-budget shields 2026, robust RMDP shielding) and adjacent deployments exist — hierarchical shielded RL for power-grid operation, recovery shields on remote microgrids, semiconductor-dispatch RL reviews citing shields — plus the Aug-2026 Safe MES filter on three electronic-production lines (0.985 replay safety, 23.6 s recovery) is the nearest manufacturing datapoint. But that filter screens *MES station decisions*, not an RL agent's *corrective-action set on a flow line with AGV/rework dynamics*; no deployed CMDP/shield/MILP-projection loop on discrete flow control with override-rate metrics exists.
- **Semester build:** Constraint spec file (invariants in checkable form) + projection layer (MILP via PuLP/OR-Tools or zonotope preimage on the 22-machine + AGV state); fault-injection suite measuring violation rate with/without shield, override rate, recovery-time delta, and shield p99 latency; compose with A1–A7 so the gate sees shield verdicts. Success = zero hard-invariant violations across suite with override rate <10% and shield p99 < 50 ms SimPy-time.
- **Falsification tripwire:** This gap closes if a paper or production report demonstrates a formally-specified shield (CMDP/shield/MILP-projection) filtering an RL/corrective-action policy on a discrete flow or job-shop line with measured violation and override rates.

### G5 — Multi-fault root separation with attribution credit (RANK 5)
- **Mechanism (3 lines):** Inject 2–3 temporally overlapping faults (e.g., wear-knee drift + AGV delay + inspection mis-classification) into topology-A replays and require the ranked trace to attribute each observed symptom to its root with credit scores (Shapley-over-walk or counterfactual leave-one-fault-out replay deltas). Report separation accuracy and misattribution rate as a function of overlap degree (lag between fault onsets, shared-path vs disjoint-path faults). Single-fault recall is the control, not the headline.
- **Closest art + why it doesn't fill it:** Simultaneous-fault diagnosis exists (MK-SVM on distillation, pairwise multi-label frameworks, residential-air MSF multi-label studies, hybrid deep multi-class models) but lives in single-equipment or process-plant settings with classifier-style outputs — not ranked causal-trace attribution over a 22-machine discrete topology with replay-grounded credit. Overlapping-symptom masking on flow lines with credit assignment is unmeasured.
- **Semester build:** Overlapping-fault scenario generator (pair taxonomy: shared-path/disjoint, staggered/simultaneous) + credit module (walk-contribution scores validated by leave-one-fault-out counterfactual replay); metric: top-k set-F1 per fault + attribution calibration; ablate single- vs multi-fault prompts/traces. Success = set-F1 ≥ 0.7 on disjoint pairs with monotonic degradation curve over overlap degree.
- **Falsification tripwire:** This gap closes if a benchmark or paper reports overlapping multi-fault RCA on a discrete line (≥2 simultaneous roots) with per-root attribution scores and overlap-graded accuracy.

### G6 — Drift-adaptive twin that stays faithful as the plant wears (RANK 6)
- **Mechanism (3 lines):** Close the loop around the twin itself: a drift monitor (Fisher-score / MCD / error-CUSUM on prediction residuals) watches SimPy-vs-plant divergence driven by slow wear (WSTATE wear-knee parameter drift) and triggers targeted recalibration (re-fit only drifted station parameters, LoRA-style low-rank update for surrogate parts) under a versioned replay-hash so every twin version is reproducible. Twin fidelity (residual KS distance, downstream RCA recall) is tracked over simulated months, not days.
- **Closest art + why it doesn't fill it:** Adaptive twins are an active 2026 front (Fisher+LoRA continual validation framework; self-improving DT with drift detection; ACL-DT sub-ms cycle prediction at ~7% error, 80× faster than DES; Kalman online geometry calibration; online Bayesian calibration under gradual/abrupt change) — but demonstrations target cycle-time prediction, geometry, or generic surrogates over short horizons, never a full discrete-manufacturing SimPy twin tracked across wear-knee months with RCA-recall-as-fidelity-metric. The "who keeps the twin faithful" operator loop is missing.
- **Semester build:** Wear-driver (months-long WSTATE drift schedule incl. knee) + monitor + recalibrator + versioned twin registry; control (frozen twin) vs adaptive twin on RCA recall@3 and residual drift over simulated 6-month horizon; cost metric: recalibration compute + samples per fidelity point. Success = adaptive twin holds recall within 5 pts of day-0 while frozen twin degrades ≥15 pts.
- **Falsification tripwire:** This gap closes if a publication demonstrates months-horizon online twin recalibration on a discrete manufacturing line where downstream RCA/diagnosis accuracy (not just prediction error) is the fidelity metric.

## Considered and REJECTED (contradiction-seeking worked)

- **R1 — CMMS work-order compiler (rejected: vendor-crowded).** 2026 vendor artifacts (Oxmaint PLC→auto-WO cutting notification to seconds; iFactory auto-WO with sensor data + priority via CMMS APIs; OSI-PI→WO pipelines) already do alarm→work-order with parts/priority fields. A narrow causal-trace→WO-with-labor/parts-estimate + outcome-feedback variant is buildable but novelty is low and positioning fights commercial feature lists. Revisit only as a thin integration chapter, not a novelty claim.
- **R2 — Approval/escalation UX as standalone gap (rejected: overlaps claimed trust gap + vendor HITL).** Gartner 10%-by-2030-with-human-approval is confirmed; vendor HITL checkpoints (Eyelit EyeQ approval workflows, Harmoni HAL, MAKA risk-aware HITL, XR teleoperation) cover the interface pattern, and Verdandi already claims operator-trust measurement. Any approval-latency/override study must be scoped as an *instrumentation appendix to the trust gap*, not a separate claim.
